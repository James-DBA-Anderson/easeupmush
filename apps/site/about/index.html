Browser games from Pompey for Pompey

What is Ease Up Mush?

Ease Up Mush is a tiny studio for hand-drawn browser games rooted in Portsmouth — Southsea seafront, Solent weather, and the scrap you clock on a Saturday night.

No app stores, no accounts, no loot boxes. Open a tab, play, close the tab. Games from Pompey, for Pompey — and anyone who likes a proper brawl.

What's here

  • Pompey Punch-Up — a crude Streets of Rage–style brawler along Southsea. Wear them down; no health bars, all body language.
  • More games on the way when they're ready to put on the pier.

Everything runs in the browser. Works on a laptop; playable on a phone in landscape with on-screen controls.

What it's not

Not a publisher portal, not a social network, not a live-service grind. No newsletter signup wall, no “create an account to continue.”

If you want polished AAA production values or a competitive ladder — this isn't that. It's doodle scrap with chip rock and seagulls.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Open the game and play.
Where does my progress live?
In your browser for that session. There's no cloud save — finish the scrap or start again.
Is it free?
Yes. If you enjoy it, you can support the work — same games for everyone, no paid tiers.
Who's behind it?
Built in Portsmouth by James Anderson. Ease Up Mush is the label on the door.

Support us

Ease Up Mush is free to play. If the games help you while away an evening — or remind you of the front — you can buy me a coffee. Same scrap for everyone. No extras locked behind a tip.
